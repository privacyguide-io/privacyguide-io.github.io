The Problem

Data Brokers Are Selling Your Life Online

Data broker websites — like Spokeo, WhitePages, BeenVerified, and 30 others — collect your personal information from public records, social media, and purchased databases. They then publish it publicly and sell it to anyone who searches your name.

This includes your current and past home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, relatives' names, estimated income, and more — often without your knowledge or consent.

These sites are used by employers, landlords, debt collectors, and people with bad intentions. Your data being out there creates real risks: unwanted contact, stalking risk, identity theft exposure, and loss of privacy.
